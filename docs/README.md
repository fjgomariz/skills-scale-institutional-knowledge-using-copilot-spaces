# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management documentation hub. This collection of guides provides a comprehensive framework for running cross-functional projects that deliver product features, services, and integrations. Whether you're a new team member getting oriented or an experienced contributor looking for specific process details, these docs will help you understand how OctoAcme approaches project delivery.

OctoAcme's project management approach is built on five core principles: customer-first prioritization, iterative delivery of small testable increments, clear ownership with named Project Managers and Product Leads, data-informed decision-making, and psychological safety that encourages feedback and learning. Projects follow a structured lifecycle from initiation through planning, execution, release, and retrospective, with each phase having specific deliverables and decision gates. The framework emphasizes lightweight artifacts like Project One-pagers, risk registers, and sprint backlogs, ensuring teams stay aligned without unnecessary overhead.

Key to our success is a well-defined communication cadence and collaboration rhythm. Teams maintain weekly syncs between Project Managers and Product Managers, conduct twice-weekly standups for delivery teams, and provide monthly stakeholder updates. During execution, we use a project board workflow with clear stages (Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, Done), enforce pull request best practices with automated CI/CD checks, and maintain quality through unit tests, integration tests, and security scanning. Our escalation paths ensure that blockers are addressed at the appropriate level—from team-level triage to sponsor escalations for business-impacting issues.

The role structure is designed for clarity and accountability. Developers implement features and maintain quality standards, Product Managers define outcomes and prioritize work based on customer value, and Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage risks, and facilitate communication across stakeholders. QA teams validate acceptance criteria, while stakeholders provide critical inputs and approvals throughout the lifecycle. This distributed ownership model, combined with regular retrospectives and continuous improvement practices, enables OctoAcme to deliver reliably while learning and adapting from each project experience.
